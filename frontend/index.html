Derivatives & Financial Abbreviations
PCR (Put-Call Ratio) PE Open Interest / CE Open Interest

Values > 1.0 indicate strong put writing / bullish support floor. Values < 0.8 indicate heavy call writing resistance.

Max Pain argmin_K [ Cumulative Loss to Option Buyers ]

The strike where option buyers experience the greatest total financial loss. Price often pins near this strike upon expiration.

ATM IV (At-The-Money Implied Volatility) Annualized Volatility (%)

Market expectation of future price swings. Higher IV expands option pricing and option premium seller yields.

Option Greeks (Δ, Γ, Θ, ν) Delta • Gamma • Theta • Vega

Delta measures directional sensitivity, Gamma measures acceleration, Theta measures daily time decay earnings, and Vega measures volatility sensitivity.
